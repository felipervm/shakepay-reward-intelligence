01

Status eligibility

Bright: CAD 200 in eligible deposits or CAD 100 in exchange activity. Blue: CAD 2,000 or CAD 1,000 respectively. These are separate qualification paths.

Payroll, pensions and supported government benefits can qualify. e-Transfers, internal transfers and crypto deposits do not.

Reward-status guidance ↗Help Center · August 31, 2026
02

Dates change the snapshot

Status activates when the monthly requirement is reached, then lasts through the next month. An event after the date being audited cannot establish status earlier.

Benefits and Rewards Terms ↗Terms · September 10, 2026
03

One deposit, different product rules

Reward-status eligibility is broader than the payroll-coded deposit required to trigger auto-buy. A pension can count toward status without triggering that payroll workflow.

Payroll auto-buy guidance ↗Help Center · March 11, 2026

03 / HOW I BUILT AND CHECKED IT

Same question.
Two implementations.

Python: contributions and review reasons

The model filters at the audit date, aggregates confirmed eligible events separately, and checks current-month qualification plus prior-month carryover. Money is calculated in integer cents.

It reports excluded events, future events and uncertain classification separately. A known status difference becomes a review case, not a diagnosis.

SQL: independently recomputed status

SQLite derives expected status directly from the event table. It does not reuse Python's calculated tier. The fixture runner checks that both implementations agree.

The larger fixture contains deliberately altered observed statuses to exercise comparison. Those differences are test inputs, not research findings.

04 / THE DECISION THIS COULD SUPPORT

Test a transaction explanation.
Not another status dashboard.

Shakepay already shows status and progress in Shaker Profile. My hypothesis is narrower: a transaction-level explanation might help someone understand an exclusion or date boundary, while reserving uncertain cases for internal review.

Validate the inputs

Agree on effective timestamps, policy dates and payment classifications. Use approved, de-identified event and status history.

Test one explanation

Compare a small transaction explanation with the existing experience, if support evidence indicates a meaningful gap.

Measure the result

Track comprehension and eligibility-related contacts per 1,000 relevant events. Review complaint levels, incorrect benefit denials and pressure to spend or trade.

Define contact categories and the measurement window before testing. A meaningful improvement, no effect, or evidence the current journey already works are all possible outcomes. No impact estimate is claimed here.
