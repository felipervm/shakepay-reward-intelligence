01 / THE QUESTIONSTARTING WITH UNCERTAINTY

One deposit.
Two possible
outcomes.

A paycheque can arrive successfully without necessarily qualifying for every reward a customer expects.

That doesn't mean the system is broken. Transaction types, month boundaries and eligibility rules can lead to different outcomes.

I wanted to know how those situations could be distinguished, before they become confusing or generate a support request.

Follow the evidence ↗

details.

Three observations shaped the analysis.

01

Eligible activity isn't the same as money received.

Published reward criteria distinguish qualifying direct deposits and exchange activity from excluded types. Related products may use different classifications.

05 / WHAT I WOULD TRY NEXTIF THE DATA SUPPORT THE HYPOTHESIS

Start small.
Measure well.

The next step isn't to rewrite reward policy. It's to understand which outcomes need a clearer explanation and which need investigation.

I'd start with one eligible workflow, reconcile the event and status history, then test a small approved change.

01

Agree on rules

Validate effective dates, transaction classifications, reversals and exceptions.

02

Audit the journey

Compare eligible events, tier changes, observed status and support contacts.

03

Clarify one outcome

Test an explanation or internal exception queue for one case category.

04

Check the effect

Measure comprehension and contacts alongside fairness and financial-risk guardrails.

The current system may already work well. That would also be a useful finding.
